Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Creator OS (“we”, “us”, or “the service”) handles information when you use Creator OS to prepare, organize, schedule, or publish content to Instagram.

1. Information we may process

Depending on the features you enable, Creator OS may process your Instagram account identifier and basic profile information made available through Meta’s APIs; access credentials or tokens needed to connect to Instagram; captions, media references, draft content and publishing schedules you provide; publishing status, post identifiers and API responses; and basic operational records such as queue activity, errors and timestamps.

2. How we use information

We use this information only to connect the Instagram account you authorize, prepare and manage content, submit publishing requests you initiate or enable, display publishing status, enforce configured scheduling and safety controls, and troubleshoot or secure the service.

3. Instagram and other service providers

When you connect Instagram, information and publishing requests are exchanged with Meta/Instagram through the permissions and APIs you authorize. If you choose to enable an automation provider such as Zapier, the content and event details required for that workflow may also be sent to that provider. Your use of those services is also subject to their own privacy terms. We do not sell personal information.

4. Storage and security

Creator OS is an evolving service. Information may be held in the application’s configured storage and deployment environment. We take reasonable steps appropriate to the current implementation, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Do not share access tokens or passwords with anyone. Production use should rely on protected secrets and access controls; do not assume that every development or self-hosted configuration provides encrypted database storage or account-level access controls.

5. Retention

We retain information only for as long as it is needed to operate the features you use, maintain relevant publishing and troubleshooting records, or meet applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion as described below. Some records may remain temporarily in backups or provider systems until their normal deletion cycles complete.

6. Your choices and deletion requests

You can stop future access by removing or revoking Creator OS’s permissions in your Meta/Instagram account settings. You can request deletion of information associated with your use of Creator OS by emailing dhaval.work97@gmail.com with the subject “Creator OS Data Deletion Request”. See our Data Deletion Instructions.

7. Children

Creator OS is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes

We may update this policy when the service or its data practices change. The effective date above indicates the latest revision.
