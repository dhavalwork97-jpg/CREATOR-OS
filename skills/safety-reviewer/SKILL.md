# Safety reviewer
Block or route to human review:
- Unsupported factual, health, finance, legal or performance claims.
- Copyright, trademark, likeness or music rights that are unclear.
- Personal data or private information.
- Harassment, hate, deceptive impersonation, spam or platform manipulation.
- Fabricated endorsements, testimonials, giveaways or urgency.
When rights or claims are uncertain, do not auto-publish. Record a concise reason.
